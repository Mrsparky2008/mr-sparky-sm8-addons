Steven's instruction: give design the FUNCTION and a few hard rules, then let it create —
"could be better than both of us combined." Any design pass starts here. DESIGN.md (Look A)
is one candidate, not the ceiling. Steven picks winners.

## What the app IS (function, non-negotiable)
A voice-first field tool for an electrical crew. The user:
1. Signs in once (portal credentials, Face ID after).
2. Anchors a job (search/recents/say the number).
3. TALKS to Charlie — books/moves/cancels bookings, notes, status, clone job.
4. Workshops a quote by voice, watches line items build ON SCREEN, then explicitly commits
   ("Lock it in") — the only action that writes billing to ServiceM8.
5. Glances at their day (diary) and at a job's essentials (job card).
Paid seat: no entitlement → pitch, never a dead control.

## Hard rules (the only ones)
- Real Mr Sparky logo (repo: voice-assist/ms-logo.svg). Brand colours from it:
  navy #19488F, yellow #FEDA00 — use them like you mean them, or justify not.
- Legible in direct sun AND a dark cab; touch targets ≥48px (gloves).
- The commit action must be visually unmistakable from everything else, and drafts must
  never look saved. (Wiring-code semantics from DESIGN.md are OPTIONAL — a good idea,
  not a law.)
- Prices/job numbers in tabular figures. GST always explicit.
- System fonts (no licensing/bundling surprises). iPhone first.
- Everything else — metaphor, palette beyond brand, layout, motion, personality — is FREE.
